=== how mishearings are treated ===

How the wake matcher handles a bad transcription (phrase = 'hey jarvis')

    engine heard                      result   confidence
    --------------------------------------------------------------------
    'hey jarvis'                      wake     1.00
    'hey  jarvis'                     wake     1.00
    'hey jervis'                      wake     0.92
    'a jarvis'                        wake     0.50
    'jarvis'                          wake     0.00
    'hey service'                     wake     0.73
    'hey darvis open notepad'         wake     0.92
    'bye jarvis'                      exit     0.67
    'goodbye jarvis'                  exit     0.60
    'hey what is the weather'         (ignored)0.60
    'open notepad'                    (ignored)0.22

    'bye jarvis' is checked before the wake phrase, so the exit word can never
    be mistaken for 'hey jarvis' — fuzzy matching only loosens the spelling,
    never the meaning.
